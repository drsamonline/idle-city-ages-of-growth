## 1. The goal

Grow a tiny village into a **Fusion Age world-city**. Citizens live, work and build on their own —
your job is to **shape the city and keep it sustainable**. Happiness is life: **if happiness ever
hits zero, the city collapses and the run is over.**

The **status bar** along the bottom always tells you exactly what is blocking growth
(*no housing capacity*, *no power*, *fuel shortage*, *unemployed citizens*, …). Read it first.

## 2. Resources

| Resource | Icon | What it does |
|---|:--:|---|
| **Money** | 💰 | Built up by Corporate zones (when residents fill their jobs). Pays for almost everything. |
| **Materials** | 🧱 | From Quarries, Industrial zones and Lumber Camps. The other half of every build cost. |
| **Food** | 🍖 | Feeds your population. Farms and Hydroponic Domes produce it; starvation tanks happiness. |
| **Fuel** | ⛽ | Wood early, then coal and oil. Powers industry and power plants. |
| **Power** | ⚡ | Needed by most buildings in later ages. Supplied by Steam/Oil/Nuclear/Solar/Fusion plants. |
| **Research** | 🔬 | Earned from scholars (libraries, universities). Spent on the TECH tab. |
| **Population** | 👥 | Citizens who move into Residential zones. They work jobs and pay taxes. |
| **Happiness** | 🙂 | The master meter. Raised by jobs, Fun, services and decor; lowered by pollution, unemployment, shortages and disasters. |

Every resource has a live **+/- per second** rate shown in the top bar.

## 3. The eight Ages

Advancing an Age unlocks new buildings and multiplies your economy.

| # | Age | Income multiplier | Highlights unlocked |
|:-:|---|:--:|---|
| 0 | **Stone Age** | ×1.0 | Roads, all four zones, Lumber Camp, Quarry, Farm, Compost Heap, Great Monolith |
| 1 | **Bronze Age** | ×1.3 | Park, Waste Depot, Bronze Ziggurat |
| 2 | **Iron Age** | ×1.6 | Watchpost, Fire Watch, Healer, Iron Citadel |
| 3 | **Medieval Age** | ×2.0 | School, Plaza, Grand Cathedral |
| 4 | **Industrial Age** | ×2.6 | Coal Mine, Steam Plant, Recycling Plant, Grand Exposition |
| 5 | **Modern Age** | ×3.5 | Oil Pump, Oil Plant, Library, Grand Fountain, Sky Needle |
| 6 | **Digital Age** | ×5.0 | Nuclear Plant, Solar Farm, Grid Battery, University, High-rise Tower, Orbital Beacon |
| 7 | **Fusion Age** | ×8.0 | Fusion Reactor, Space Elevator |

**To advance an Age you must complete that Age's quests.** The **ADVANCE AGE** button in the side
panel lights up when the set is done.

## 4. Building your city

### 🛣 ROAD
- **Road** — buildings only work when a road connects them back to the **Town Hall**.
  Pick it and **drag to paint** a line of roads.
- **Demolish** — remove a building for a **25 % refund**.

### 🏠 ZONES
Zones cost materials and money once, then **build, fill and level up by themselves — for free**.
- **Residential** — houses citizens. Capacity and looks grow with level and Age.
- **Corporate** — shops and offices. Earns money when residents fill the jobs.
- **Industrial** — produces materials; needs power (later ages) and fuel; pollutes.
- **Fun District** — parks, theatres, stadiums. Raises happiness.
- **High-rise Tower** — dense housing, unlocked by the *Skyscrapers* tech (Digital Age).

### ⚡ POWER / FUEL
- **Lumber Camp** — fuel (wood) plus a little materials.
- **Quarry** — steady materials.
- **Farm / Hydroponic Dome** — food.
- **Coal Mine · Oil Pump** — big fuel production (and pollution).
- **Steam Plant · Oil Plant · Nuclear Plant · Solar Farm · Fusion Reactor** — electricity.
  Everything except Solar and Fusion burns fuel; keep an eye on the ⚡ meter.
- **Grid Battery** — stores power to smooth over spikes.

### 🏥 SERVICES
Coverage buildings that improve life within a radius: **Watchpost** (safety), **Fire Watch**
(fire response — halves fire downtime), **Healer**, **School**, **Library**, **University**
(research), **Compost Heap**, **Waste Depot**, **Recycling Plant** (waste). The **Town Hall**
is the heart of the city — roads must reach it.

### 🌳 DECOR
**Park · Plaza · Grand Fountain** — cheap happiness, no jobs, no pollution.

### 🏛 WONDERS
One-off landmark builds (Great Monolith → Space Elevator) with big happiness and prestige effects.
Each Age has its own wonder.

### 🔬 TECH
Research tab. Spend 🔬 on 10 techs that reshape the economy:

| Tech | Age | Cost | Effect |
|---|:-:|--:|---|
| Writing | 1 | 20 | +25 % research from all sources |
| Crop Rotation | 1 | 30 | +25 % food from farms & domes |
| Currency | 2 | 60 | +15 % city income |
| Engineering | 3 | 120 | Roads cost 30 % less |
| Medicine | 4 | 220 | +5 happiness, +10 % growth |
| Power Grid | 5 | 350 | +20 % power capacity |
| Zero Waste | 5 | 400 | Citizens produce 20 % less waste |
| Mass Transit | 6 | 600 | Roads carry 40 % more traffic |
| Skyscrapers | 6 | 800 | Unlocks the High-rise Tower zone |
| AI Administration | 7 | 1500 | +20 % city income |

## 6. Challenges 🎯

Timed optional challenges from the 🎯 button. Beat the deadline for a **gold** medal; the looser
limit earns **silver**. Medals are kept per save.

| Challenge | Objective | Deadline | 🥇 Gold | 🥈 Silver |
|---|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 🏘 **Boom Town** | Reach 100 population | 15 min | 480 | 720 |
| 💰 **Tycoon** | Hold 25,000 💰 at once | 20 min | 600 | 900 |
| 🌿 **Green City** | Reach 150 population with waste under 10 | 30 min | 900 | 1500 |

## 7. Events, weather & disasters

**Random events** (age-gated): 🎪 Festival, 📦 Supply convoy, 💰 Trade boom, 👷 Migration wave,
🔥 Fire scare, ⚡ Grid strain. Each has an upside and, sometimes, a cost.

**Weather** cycles automatically:
| | Effect |
|---|---|
| ☀️ Clear | none |
| 🌧 Rain | farms **+30 % food** |
| ❄️ Snow | growth **−20 %**, happiness **−3** |
| 🔥 Heat wave | power output **−10 %** |

**Disasters** strike without warning: 🔥 **Fire** (knocks a building offline until repaired — a
Fire Watch halves the downtime) and 🌋 **Earthquake** (damages several buildings at once).

**Citizen petitions** 📜 appear in the side panel — fund a festival, a clinic day, a community
garden, cobblestone repairs or a library for a happiness boost, or raise council taxes for a cash
injection at the cost of goodwill.

## 8. Saving, exporting & Ascension

- **3 save slots** (💾 button) plus **autosave** every 10 seconds.
- **Export / Import**: copy the save code to move your city to another device or back it up.
- **🌟 Ascend**: in the **Fusion Age** with **2,500+ population**, ascend to start a fresh city
  while keeping a permanent **Legacy** bonus (+25 % income per ascension).

## 9. Tips for a healthy city

1. **Roads first.** A beautiful zone with no road does nothing. Paint roads outward from the Hall.
2. **Balance the four zones.** Too many houses and no jobs → unemployment. Too much industry and
   no Fun → unhappiness and pollution.
3. **Watch the status bar, not the map.** It names the single thing throttling growth right now.
4. **Bank power before an Age jump.** New ages switch many buildings to needing ⚡.
5. **Services are cheap happiness.** A Park or Plaza often fixes a mood slump faster than zoning.
6. **Use ⏸ / 1x / 2x / 4x / 8x** to speed through quiet stretches and pause while you plan.
7. **Don't ignore waste.** Waste and pollution quietly drain happiness until the city tips over.
